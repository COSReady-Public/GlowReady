# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026)

First release.

- Before-and-after editor: add photos by clicking or dropping onto the preview
- 3 formats: Post 1080 × 1350, Square 1080 × 1080, Story 1080 × 1920
- 6 styles: Classic, Polaroid, Overlap, Diagonal, Magazine, Blend
- 19 service presets plus Timeline (Day 0 / Today), with per-industry disclaimers
- Studio name or Instagram handle on every export
- Drag, pinch, scroll and double-tap framing, plus keyboard controls
- Photo adjustments: Exposure, Contrast, Lighting, Saturation, Warmth
- Decor layer: corner flourishes and a dot texture, with a switch
- Anonymous, cookieless export counter that respects Do Not Track
